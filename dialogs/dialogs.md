**INT. TOWN BAR — DAY**

_Player pushes through the saloon doors. The bartender looks up._

**BARTENDER** You the new sheriff they sent?

_Player nods._

**BARTENDER** Well, you can turn right back around. They already sent one before you. Didn't end well.

_Player looks confused._

**BARTENDER** What, they didn't tell you about her? A woman, sent here to do exactly what you're about to do. Those things out there made quick work of her.

**BARTENDER** _(quieter)_ We managed to pull her out while they weren't looking. But I don't think she's gonna make it.

**BARTENDER** Anyway — if you still wanna do this after hearing that, I've got some information that might help.

**[CHOICE: Do it / Turn back and leave]** _(Leaving ends the game.)_

---

**IF PLAYER STAYS:**

**BARTENDER** _(a faint smirk)_ Huh. Guess you've got some meat on your bones after all.

**BARTENDER** I happen to know one of the monsters. Calls himself Cow Cact now. Used to be a regular in here — fastest gunslinger this town ever saw. Man didn't want anybody messing with him, or his hat. _Especially_ the hat.

**BARTENDER** You go up against him, you'd better have fast hands. Slow ones just end up buried.

---

**[BOSS FIGHT: COW CACT]**

**IF PLAYER WINS:**

**BARTENDER** _(setting down the glass he'd been wiping)_ ...To think I ever doubted you.

**IF PLAYER didn't fight yet:**

**BARTENDER** That's all I know.

---

**INT. CEMETERY — LATER**

_The Gravedigger watching the player approach._

**IF COW CACT IS DEFEATED:**

**GRAVEDIGGER** Heard you got one of 'em.

**GRAVEDIGGER** _(shaking his head) Never thought I'd live long enough to see this town breathe free again.

**GRAVEDIGGER** ...I've got some information on that damn loose coffin roaming the outskirts. Might seem impossible to put down, but —

**GRAVEDIGGER** Hit the hole in the coffin. That ought to do enough damage to send him back where he came from.

**GRAVEDIGGER** Six feet under. Where he belongs.

**IF COW CACT IS NOT YET DEFEATED:**

**GRAVEDIGGER** Get him first, and I might have some information on that handsome cat who caused all this.

---

**[BOSS FIGHT: THE LOOSE COFFIN]**

**IF PLAYER WINS:**

**GRAVEDIGGER** Well, well. Just as I expected — from someone who could take down my fastest gunslinger.

_Player looks confused. The Gravedigger's grin stretches too wide, too long. His form buckles, twists — and the Coyote stands in his place._

**COYOTE** _(screaming, feral)_ I won't let you take this town from me!

---

**[BOSS FIGHT: COYOTE]**

**IF PLAYER WINS:**

_The Coyote collapses into dust, Silence settles over the graveyard for the first time in years._

**COYOTE** _(voice already fading) This town was mine... I built it from nothing...

_The last of him scatters into the dirt._

**[Beat.]**


**BARTENDER** _(to himself)_ ...Free.

**[TOWN THEME shifts — the dust settles, lights flicker on down main street. FADE OUT.]**

**IF PLAYER LOSES:**

_The Coyote looms over the fallen player_

**COYOTE** Should've turned back when you had the chance.

**[GAME OVER.]**
